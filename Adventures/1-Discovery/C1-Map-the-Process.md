# C1 — Map the process 🗺️ (Workflow · Discovery)

**Duration: 30 min · Copilot mode: Ask · No code to write**

## Context
Read `docs/current-returns-process.md`. Customer care at Maison Orélia loses 2 hours a day on manual sorting. Before automating anything, you must **understand and draw** the process.

## Mission
1. Ask Copilot to turn the text into a **Mermaid diagram** (`flowchart`). Preview it (the Markdown Mermaid extension is preinstalled in the Codespace).
2. Have it list the **pain points** and rank them by impact / effort.
3. Have it propose **3 steps that can be automated** and **2 that must stay human**. Justify.

## Copilot hints
- `Turn #file:docs/current-returns-process.md into a Mermaid flowchart with one lane per actor.`
- `Which steps are good candidates for automation, which are not, and why?`
- `Write the target process in 6 steps maximum.`

## Success criteria
- A readable diagram saved in `my-work/current-process.md`.
- A target process with **one clearly identified human validation step**.
- You challenged at least one Copilot suggestion.

## Bonus
Estimate the time saved in hours per week, stating your assumptions.
