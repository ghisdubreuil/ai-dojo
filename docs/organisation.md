# Organisation of Maison Orélia (fictional)

The Maison is organised as a **matrix**: 3 divisions (product) × 6 regions (market).

## 3 divisions
| Division | Categories | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| **Fashion** | Leather goods, Ready-to-wear, Accessories | €420 – €3,900 |
| **Fragrance & Beauty** | Fragrance, Skincare, Makeup | €48 – €290 |
| **Watches & Fine Jewelry** | Watches, Fine jewelry | €4,200 – €14,500 |

Each division has a global head (product strategy, margins, assortment).

## 6 regions
| Region | Stores |
|---|---|
| **UK** | London Mayfair |
| **US** | New York Madison, Los Angeles Rodeo |
| **APAC** | Singapore Orchard, Hong Kong Landmark |
| **Japan** | Tokyo Ginza, Osaka Shinsaibashi |
| **Europe** | Paris Faubourg, Lyon Presqu'île, Milan Montenapoleone |
| **Swiss & Others** | Geneva Rues-Basses, Dubai Mall |

Each region has a regional head (sales, stores, local customer care). **Japan** is separate from APAC (specific market, clientele and operations).

## Where the two axes meet
- **Revenue and margin** are read by division **and** by region.
- **Customer care** is run locally by region, with product expertise by division (leather goods workshop, watchmaking workshop, jewelry workshop).
- **Rules** (returns, warranties) are global, with **regional exceptions** (see `knowledge-base/returns-policy-v3.md`).
