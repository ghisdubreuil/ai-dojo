# Returns and exchanges policy — v3 (January 2026)

> Maison Orélia is a **fictional** house created for this dojo.

## Time limits
- Return or exchange: **30 days** after delivery (e-commerce) or purchase (store).
- Refund to the original payment method within **14 days** after the returned item is received.

## Exclusions
- **Opened** fragrance and skincare products: no return.
- Personalised items (monogram): no return, repair only.

## Regional exceptions
- **Japan**: original packaging and tags required; **30-day** limit.
- **US**: in-store returns are refunded; returns by mail are refunded as store credit.
- **UK**: **30-day** limit, refund within **14 days**.
- **Swiss & Others**: **30-day** limit; return shipping costs are paid by the customer outside Switzerland.
- **Europe** and **APAC**: general rule.

## Watches & Fine Jewelry
- Watches and fine jewelry: return possible within **14 days** (not 30), item **unworn**, with certificate and packaging intact.
- Resized or engraved item: no return, repair only.

## Special cases
- Item received broken: photo required within **48 hours**, free replacement.
- Gift: exchange only, against a credit note valid for 12 months.
