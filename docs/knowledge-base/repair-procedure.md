# Customer care procedure — repairs

1. The store or the customer creates a repair request with photos.
2. The workshop assesses within **5 working days**: repairable / not repairable.
3. A quote is sent if out of warranty. Warranty: **2 years** on manufacturing defects (leather goods).
4. Average repair time for leather goods: **6 to 8 weeks**.
5. If not repairable under warranty: exchange for an equivalent value, approved by the customer care manager.

## Watches and fine jewelry
- **5-year** warranty on watches (movement), **2 years** on jewelry.
- Items are sent to the central workshop in Geneva; repair time for a watch: **8 to 12 weeks**.
- Any item worth more than €2,000 is shipped with an insured carrier.

## Escalation
- Unhappy customer or value above €2,000: escalate to the customer care manager within 24 h.
- VIC (priority customer): reply within 4 working hours.
