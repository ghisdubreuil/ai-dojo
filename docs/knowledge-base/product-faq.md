# Product FAQ (extracts)

## Fragrance
- Bottles of 50 ml and 100 ml are allowed in cabin luggage if 100 ml or less.
- Shelf life: 36 months after opening.

## Leather goods
- Full-grain leather: avoid water, care with the supplied kit.
- Aubrac Bag: 2-year warranty on handles.

## Watches
- Water resistance: 30 m, to be checked every 2 years. Avoid magnetic fields.
- A gain or loss of more than 1 minute per day justifies a warranty check.

## Fine jewelry
- Engraving and resizing available, 3 to 4 weeks, no return possible after the work is done.

## Skincare
- Radiance Serum: do not apply on broken skin.
