# Tone guide — customer replies

- Formal address, short sentences, never an exclamation mark.
- Never promise a firm delivery date without customer care approval.
- Do not use: "unfortunately", "as already stated", "your problem".
- Do use: "We understand", "We are taking care of it", the customer's first name when known.
- Signature: "The Customer Relations team, Maison Orélia".
- Japan: more ceremonial phrasing, open with thanks. US and UK: slightly more direct.
- Any reply about an urgent gift is prioritised.
